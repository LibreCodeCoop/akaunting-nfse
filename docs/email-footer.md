# NFS-e email delivery and branding

NFS-e issuance notifications use a company-specific LibreCode footer instead of
the promotional email footer inherited from the Akaunting Core.

Administrators with `update-nfse-settings` can edit the plain-text promotional
message and the HTTPS destination in **NFS-e > Settings > NFS-e email footer**.
Both settings are stored under the active company, and the queue worker restores
that company context before rendering. HTML in the message is escaped and URLs
must be HTTPS without embedded credentials. There are no tracking pixels or
external images.

This affects only notifications generated by the NFS-e module. Standard
Akaunting invoice/payment emails still use Core branding and require a
separate customization strategy.

When a sender requests the invoice PDF, DANFSE or authorized XML, the email
must not be delivered if any requested attachment cannot be prepared.
For NFS-e, the email first uses the immutable authorized XML saved with that
receipt. It can render DANFSE locally from the XML, without an external WebDAV
read or a second fiscal issuance. Historical receipts may still retrieve their
persisted artifacts from WebDAV, without guessing remote keys.
The email job records a failed delivery rather than silently marking an
incomplete message successful. Failed/sent emails must not automatically
be resent; only the email-delivery operation may be retried after checking
whether the first attempt was delivered. Never repeat a fiscal issuance.
